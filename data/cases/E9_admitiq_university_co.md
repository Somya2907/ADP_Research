# E9 — AdmitIQ University Admissions

## Metadata
- jurisdiction: CO
- doctrinal_area: high-risk AI in education, consumer notice, correction and appeal
- statutes: CO AIA (SB 24-205) §6-1-1701 through §6-1-1707
- tier: easy
- role: test
- parties: Pinecrest Online University, AdmitIQ, Lumina Learning Analytics

## Facts

Pinecrest Online University is a private university based in Aurora, Colorado, with 620 full-time employees. It enrolls students from across Colorado.

Pinecrest uses "AdmitIQ," a system licensed from Lumina Learning Analytics, to decide undergraduate admission and to award merit scholarships. AdmitIQ scores each application. Applications that score below a fixed cutoff are denied automatically, and scholarship amounts are set directly from the score. No admissions officer reviews a denial. Lumina trained AdmitIQ on its own multi-institution dataset; Pinecrest has not supplied its own data for training.

Pinecrest has taken two compliance steps. In January 2026 it adopted a written AI risk management program aligned with ISO/IEC 42001. In March 2026 it completed an impact assessment of AdmitIQ covering the system's purpose, data categories, discrimination risks, outputs, limitations, and monitoring.

Pinecrest has not taken the following steps:
- Its website carries no statement about AdmitIQ or about how discrimination risks are managed.
- Applicants are not told before a decision that an AI system will make it. They learn this only from the denial letter, which says "an automated system was used in reviewing your application."
- Applicants have no way to correct personal data that AdmitIQ used.
- The denial letter states that admission decisions are "final and not subject to review."
