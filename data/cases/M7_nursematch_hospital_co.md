# M7 — NurseMatch Hospital Hiring

## Metadata
- jurisdiction: CO
- doctrinal_area: preparatory-task exclusion, substantial factor, impact assessment timing
- statutes: CO AIA (SB 24-205) §6-1-1701 through §6-1-1707
- tier: medium
- role: test
- parties: St. Brendan Regional Health, NurseMatch, Altura Workforce

## Facts

St. Brendan Regional Health is a hospital system in Denver, Colorado, with 1,900 full-time employees.

Since early 2025 St. Brendan has used "NurseMatch," a system licensed from Altura Workforce, when hiring nurses. NurseMatch scores every applicant and produces a shortlist containing the top 20%. Recruiters interview only applicants on the shortlist, and a hiring manager chooses among those interviewed. In the fourth quarter of 2025, 91% of nurses hired came from the top tenth of NurseMatch scores, and no applicant outside the shortlist was hired.

St. Brendan considers NurseMatch outside the law: "It performs a preparatory task. People conduct the interviews and people decide."

St. Brendan's compliance measures are:
- A risk management program modeled on the NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0, adopted in February 2025, with a governance committee.
- An impact assessment of NurseMatch completed in February 2025. It has not been updated since.
- In November 2025, Altura retrained NurseMatch on new data and changed several scoring features, releasing it as "version 3." St. Brendan did not perform a new impact assessment.
- No review of the deployment for discriminatory outcomes has been done since February 2025.
- A statement about NurseMatch and how its risks are managed is posted on the careers section of the website.
- Applicants first learn that an AI system was used from the rejection email, sent after the decision.
- An applicant may email HR to correct résumé information. There is no way to appeal a rejection.

## Question

Assess St. Brendan's compliance position under the Colorado AI Act (SB 24-205) as of the expected effective date (June 30, 2026). Specifically address: (1) Whether NurseMatch is a "high-risk artificial intelligence system," including whether the preparatory-task exclusion applies; (2) Whether NurseMatch is a "substantial factor" given that people interview and decide; (3) Whether the impact assessment is current, considering the annual requirement, the November 2025 changes, and the rule for systems deployed before the effective date; (4) Whether the timing and content of the applicant notice are sufficient; (5) What obligations remain, including appeal. Use the F-I-R-A-C-O structure.
