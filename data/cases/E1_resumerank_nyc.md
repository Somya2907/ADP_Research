## Facts

HireFlow Inc., a Series B talent-acquisition software company based in Austin, Texas, sells "ResumeRank," a machine-learning-based résumé screening tool that scores candidates 1–100 on predicted role fit. The tool uses natural language processing to parse résumé text and a gradient-boosted model trained on historical hiring outcomes to generate scores. Candidates scoring above 70 are forwarded to hiring managers; those below 70 are automatically rejected.

Greenleaf Foods, a national grocery chain with its headquarters in Chicago and 12,000 employees nationwide, licensed ResumeRank in March 2025 and has been using it to screen applicants for its six retail locations in Brooklyn and Manhattan. Greenleaf's HR department relies on ResumeRank as the sole initial screen for all hourly retail positions at these NYC locations.

ResumeRank was last independently audited for bias by TalentMetrics LLC, a consulting firm that is also HireFlow's contracted vendor partner for ongoing model monitoring, in November 2023. HireFlow substantially retrained the ResumeRank model in June 2024 using updated training data, but did not commission a new bias audit after the retrain.

Greenleaf has not posted any bias audit summary on its website. Greenleaf's NYC job postings on its careers page and on Indeed.com include no mention of AEDT use. No notice of any kind regarding automated screening is provided to candidates before or during the application process.

A candidate who applied for a cashier position at the Brooklyn location in January 2026 was rejected without interview. The candidate recently filed a complaint with the NYC Department of Consumer and Worker Protection (DCWP), alleging that an automated tool was used without required disclosure.
