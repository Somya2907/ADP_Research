## Facts

Metropolitan Health System operates 14 hospitals and clinics across all five boroughs of New York City. It employs approximately 22,000 staff. Metropolitan uses "PromoteAI," an internal algorithmic tool custom-built by its IT department in 2022, to rank candidates for nursing supervisor promotions. PromoteAI ingests employee performance review scores, years of experience, patient satisfaction ratings, and continuing education records, then generates a composite promotion-readiness score for each candidate on a scale of 0–100.

Metropolitan's General Counsel commissioned a bias audit of PromoteAI in January 2025 from Compliance Analytics Group, an independent consulting firm that does no other work for Metropolitan Health System and has no financial relationship with the organization. The audit was completed in March 2025 and evaluated PromoteAI's impact on selection rates across sex, race/ethnicity, and intersectional categories. The audit summary was posted on Metropolitan's public careers page under a clearly labeled "Bias Audit Results" section in April 2025.

However, the notice sent to internal candidates eligible for promotion references only that "promotion decisions may be informed by data analytics tools," without naming PromoteAI or specifying the job qualifications and characteristics that the tool evaluates. This notice is sent via internal email three business days before the promotion evaluation cycle begins.

No alternative evaluation process is offered to candidates who wish to opt out of automated assessment. Metropolitan has not updated the bias audit since March 2025, and the next promotion cycle using PromoteAI is scheduled for May 2026.
