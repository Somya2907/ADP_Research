# M9 — CareerGauge Bank Hiring and Promotion

## Metadata
- jurisdiction: NYC
- doctrinal_area: grouped bias audit, audit currency after retraining, notice timing for candidates and employees
- statutes: NYC LL 144 §20-870 et seq., DCWP Final Rules
- tier: medium
- role: test
- parties: Hudson Commonwealth Bank, CareerGauge, Trellis People Analytics, Baines Stratton

## Facts

Hudson Commonwealth Bank employs 5,200 people in New York City. It uses "CareerGauge," a tool licensed from Trellis People Analytics, for two purposes: screening external applicants for branch analyst positions, and screening current employees for promotion to branch manager. In both uses, CareerGauge produces a score and only the top-scoring third advance.

A single bias audit covers both positions as one group. It was completed on May 12, 2025 by Baines Stratton LLP, an auditing firm with no financial or employment ties to the bank or to Trellis and no role in building the tool. The audit reports selection rates and impact ratios for sex, race/ethnicity, and intersectional categories.

In July 2025, Trellis retrained CareerGauge on updated data and added new scoring features. No new bias audit was performed.

The bank used CareerGauge for an analyst hiring cycle in March 2026. It plans to use it for a promotion cycle in June 2026.

A summary of the May 2025 audit and CareerGauge's distribution date are posted in a clearly labeled section of the bank's careers page.

For the March hiring cycle, the bank emailed a notice to candidates on Monday, March 2, 2026, and screening began on Thursday, March 12, 2026. The bank's HR team describes this as "ten days' notice." The notice names the tool, lists the qualifications it assesses, and explains how to request an alternative selection process.

For promotions, the bank relies on a written policy distributed to all employees in January 2026. The policy says CareerGauge will be used in promotion decisions and explains how to request an alternative process, but it does not identify the job qualifications or characteristics the tool assesses.

## Question

Assess the bank's compliance position under applicable US AI law as of April 2026. Specifically address: (1) Whether one bias audit may cover two positions; (2) The effect of the July 2025 retraining on the audit; (3) Whether the audit was current for the March 2026 hiring cycle and will be current for the June 2026 promotion cycle; (4) Whether the timing and content of notice were sufficient for candidates and for employees; (5) The bank's penalty exposure and the obligations it must undertake. Use the F-I-R-A-C-O structure.
