# E5 — RateSense Auto Insurance

- tier: easy
- role: training
- parties: Front Range Mutual, RateSense, Kestrel Data

## Facts

Front Range Mutual is an auto insurer headquartered in Denver, Colorado, with 310 full-time employees. It sells policies only to Colorado residents.

Since August 2025, Front Range Mutual has used "RateSense," a machine-learning system licensed from Kestrel Data, for two purposes: setting the premium each policyholder pays, and deciding whether a policy is renewed or non-renewed at the end of its term. RateSense makes both decisions automatically. No employee reviews an individual premium or non-renewal decision before it is sent to the policyholder. RateSense was trained by Kestrel Data on a national dataset; Front Range Mutual has not supplied its own data for training.

Front Range Mutual's current practices are as follows:
- It has no written policy or program for managing the risks of its AI systems.
- It has never completed an impact assessment of RateSense.
- Its website does not mention RateSense or any use of artificial intelligence.
- Policyholders are not told, before or after a decision, that an AI system set their premium or decided their renewal.
- There is no process for a policyholder to correct personal data that RateSense used.
- A policyholder who disagrees with a decision can call the general customer-service line, but service agents have no authority to change a RateSense decision.

Front Range Mutual plans no changes before June 30, 2026. Its compliance officer has stated: "Insurance pricing is already regulated by the Division of Insurance, so the AI Act adds nothing for us."
