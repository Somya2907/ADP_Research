## Facts

Northlake Software is a 900-employee company headquartered in Seattle, Washington. It has no office in New York. Northlake hires fully remote customer-support staff nationwide and uses "FitScreen," a tool licensed from Pallas Analytics, to score recorded one-way video interviews. FitScreen produces a score from 1 to 100 for each applicant. Applicants scoring below 60 are rejected automatically, with no human review of their interview.

Between June 2025 and March 2026, 212 applicants who reside in New York City were screened by FitScreen for remote positions.

Northlake commissioned a bias audit of FitScreen from Ardent Assurance LLP, which has no relationship with Northlake or Pallas and played no part in building the tool. The audit was completed in September 2025 and reports selection rates and impact ratios for sex, race/ethnicity, and intersectional categories. Northlake kept the audit report internal. No summary of the results and no distribution date for FitScreen appear anywhere on Northlake's website.

Northlake's careers page says only that "we use modern assessment technology in our hiring process." Applicants are not told, ten business days in advance or at all, that an automated tool will score their interview, which qualifications and characteristics it assesses, or how to request an alternative selection process or accommodation.

Northlake's head of HR has stated: "Local Law 144 applies to New York City employers. We don't have an office there, so it doesn't apply to us." In March 2026, a rejected applicant who lives in Brooklyn filed a complaint with the NYC Department of Consumer and Worker Protection (DCWP).
