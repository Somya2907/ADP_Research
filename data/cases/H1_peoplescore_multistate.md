# H1 — PeopleScore Multi-State

## Metadata
- jurisdiction: NYC, CO, TX
- doctrinal_area: multi-jurisdictional AEDT + consequential decision + TRAIGA
- statutes: NYC LL 144 §20-870 et seq., CO AIA §6-1-1701–1707, TX TRAIGA (HB 149)
- tier: hard
- role: training

## Facts

TalentMatrix Inc. (headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia; 800 employees) sells "PeopleScore," an AI-based candidate evaluation platform used by Fortune 500 enterprise customers across the United States. PeopleScore uses a proprietary ensemble model combining NLP analysis of résumés, structured interview scoring, and predictive performance modeling to generate a composite candidate score from 0 to 100 and a hiring recommendation (Strong Hire / Hire / No Hire).

One of TalentMatrix's largest customers is RetailCo, a national big-box retailer headquartered in Dallas, Texas with 85,000 total employees. RetailCo uses PeopleScore to evaluate candidates for hourly retail associate positions in:
(a) 400 stores across Texas (PeopleScore deployed since 2024)
(b) 80 stores in Colorado (PeopleScore deployed since March 2026)
(c) 30 stores in the five boroughs of New York City (PeopleScore deployed since 2023)

RetailCo's current compliance posture:

1. Bias audit: PeopleScore was last bias-audited in August 2025. The audit was conducted by TM Analytics, LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of TalentMatrix Inc. The audit covered selection rates across sex, race/ethnicity, and intersectional categories using RetailCo's 2024–2025 historical hiring data. Results showed impact ratios above 0.80 for all categories.

2. Public disclosure: RetailCo posts the bias audit summary on its national careers website under a tab labeled "NYC Applicants — Bias Audit Results." The summary is not visible from the main careers page or from job listings; candidates must navigate to a sub-page specifically labeled for NYC.

3. Candidate notice: RetailCo uses a boilerplate notice sent to all candidates nationally at the time of application submission, stating: "RetailCo may use technology-assisted evaluation tools in our hiring process." The notice does not name PeopleScore, does not specify which job qualifications or characteristics are evaluated, and does not inform candidates of their right to request an alternative selection process.

4. Colorado-specific measures: RetailCo completed an impact assessment in December 2025. The impact assessment document references "compliance with NIST 800-53 and the EU AI Act" as the governing frameworks. RetailCo has not completed any Colorado-specific impact assessment referencing the Colorado AI Act. RetailCo has not issued any consumer notification to Colorado applicants regarding AI-assisted hiring decisions. No appeal process or data-correction mechanism exists for Colorado applicants.

5. Texas-specific measures: No Texas-specific compliance measures have been implemented. RetailCo's general counsel has stated internally that "TRAIGA doesn't really apply to our hiring process because it only covers intentional discrimination and we don't intend to discriminate."

6. Risk management: RetailCo does not have a written AI risk management policy. RetailCo has not aligned its AI governance with the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (NIST AI 100-1) or ISO/IEC 42001.

## Question

Provide a jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction compliance assessment of RetailCo's use of PeopleScore as of April 2026. For each jurisdiction (NYC, CO, TX), identify: (i) whether applicable state or local AI law applies and on what timeline; (ii) what specific obligations are owed by RetailCo (as deployer) and TalentMatrix (as developer); (iii) what is currently missing or out of compliance; (iv) what specific remediation is required and by when. Also address: (v) whether TM Analytics qualifies as an "independent auditor" under NYC LL 144; (vi) whether NIST 800-53 satisfies the Colorado AIA's risk management framework requirement; (vii) whether the general counsel's position on TRAIGA is legally correct. Conclude with a priority-ordered action list. Use the F-I-R-A-C-O structure.
