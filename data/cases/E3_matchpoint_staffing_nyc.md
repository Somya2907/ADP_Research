## Facts

StaffBridge Partners is a staffing agency based in Jersey City, New Jersey. It recruits temporary warehouse and office workers and places them with client employers. Since September 2024, StaffBridge has used "MatchPoint," a machine-learning ranking tool licensed from Corvane Systems. For each job order, MatchPoint scores every applicant from 0 to 100 and returns a ranked shortlist. StaffBridge recruiters forward only the ten highest-scoring applicants to the client; all other applicants are never contacted.

About 60% of StaffBridge's job orders are for positions physically located in Queens and the Bronx, most of them for its largest client, Harborline Logistics. In February 2026 alone, MatchPoint ranked 1,340 candidates who live in New York City for Harborline positions.

No bias audit of MatchPoint has ever been conducted. Corvane's sales brochure states that the tool is "bias-tested internally," but no audit report exists and nothing about an audit appears on StaffBridge's website. StaffBridge's operations director has said that audits are "the vendor's job," and that New York City's rules do not reach StaffBridge because it is a New Jersey company and is "only the recruiter, not the employer."

Applicants apply through a web form. They receive no notice that an automated tool will rank them, no description of the qualifications the tool assesses, and no instructions for requesting an alternative selection process or accommodation. StaffBridge's website says nothing about what data MatchPoint collects, where the data comes from, or how long it is retained.
