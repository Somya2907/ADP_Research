## Facts

The Texas Department of Licensing Services is a state agency that issues and renews occupational licenses for individuals such as electricians, cosmetologists, and tow-truck operators.

In January 2026 the agency deployed "AskTexLicense," an artificial intelligence chatbot built by Brazos AI, on its website and its telephone line. The chatbot answers questions from individual Texas residents about license renewal deadlines, fees, and eligibility requirements. It handles about 40,000 conversations each month.

The chatbot introduces itself as "Riley, your licensing assistant" and displays a photograph of a person. At no point before or during a conversation is the resident told that Riley is an artificial intelligence system. The agency's website terms of use do not mention artificial intelligence.

The agency's IT director has said that a disclosure is unnecessary because "the answers are accurate, and people would trust it less if they knew."

On March 20, 2026, the Texas Attorney General sent the agency a written notice of an alleged violation concerning the chatbot. As of mid-April 2026 the agency has not changed how the chatbot presents itself. The agency has no AI governance program and has not aligned its practices with the NIST AI Risk Management Framework. There is no evidence that the chatbot was built or deployed with any intent to discriminate or to cause harm.
