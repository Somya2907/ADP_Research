# M3 — CareQueue Clinic Intake

## Metadata
- jurisdiction: CO
- doctrinal_area: high-risk AI in health care, small-deployer exception and its limits
- statutes: CO AIA (SB 24-205) §6-1-1701 through §6-1-1707
- tier: medium
- role: training
- parties: Arkansas Valley Physical Therapy, CareQueue, Solace Health Tech

## Facts

Arkansas Valley Physical Therapy operates five outpatient clinics around Pueblo, Colorado, and has 34 full-time employees.

Since October 2025 the clinics have used "CareQueue," an off-the-shelf system sold by Solace Health Tech. For each new patient referral, CareQueue recommends whether the clinic should accept the patient and how many therapy sessions to authorize under the clinic's self-pay plans. Front-desk staff can depart from the recommendation, but a review of referrals from October 2025 through March 2026 shows they followed it in 90% of cases, and no declined referral was ever reconsidered.

CareQueue was trained by Solace on a national dataset. To date, Arkansas Valley has not supplied any of its own data to train or tune the system. However, the renewal contract that takes effect on September 1, 2026 contains a "data partnership" clause: Arkansas Valley will send its patient outcome records to Solace so that the model can be "customized for Arkansas Valley's patient population."

Solace has given Arkansas Valley a documentation packet describing CareQueue's intended uses, known limitations, and training data.

Arkansas Valley's owner believes the business is exempt from the Colorado AI Act: "We have fewer than fifty employees. The small-business exception takes us out of the law." On that basis the clinics have:
- no AI risk management policy or program;
- no impact assessment;
- no statement on their website about CareQueue;
- a patient brochure that says only "we use scheduling software to manage appointments";
- no process for a patient to correct personal data or to appeal a declined referral;
- never reviewed whether CareQueue's recommendations disadvantage any group of patients.

## Question

Assess Arkansas Valley's compliance position under the Colorado AI Act (SB 24-205) as of the expected effective date (June 30, 2026). Specifically address: (1) Whether CareQueue is a "high-risk artificial intelligence system," including whether staff review changes that conclusion; (2) Whether the small-deployer exception applies, analyzing each element, and exactly which obligations it removes and which remain; (3) What effect the September 2026 "data partnership" clause will have; (4) Whether the owner's position is legally correct; (5) What obligations apply and by when. Use the F-I-R-A-C-O structure.
