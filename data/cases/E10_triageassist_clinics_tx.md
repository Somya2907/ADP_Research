# E10 — TriageAssist Clinic Disclosure

- tier: easy
- role: test
- parties: Lone Star Family Clinics, TriageAssist, MedVector

## Facts

Lone Star Family Clinics operates 22 primary-care clinics in San Antonio and Austin, Texas. It is a private health care service provider.

Since February 2026, every clinic has used "TriageAssist," an artificial intelligence system built by MedVector. When a patient checks in and enters symptoms on a tablet, TriageAssist analyzes them and gives the treating physician a recommended visit priority and a list of suggested diagnostic tests. Clinic records show that physicians adopt the suggested tests in most visits.

Patients are not told that TriageAssist is used. The check-in screen, the consent forms, and the visit summary say nothing about artificial intelligence. Lone Star's chief medical officer has given two reasons: that a disclosure "would only confuse patients," and that because a physician signs off on every order, the AI system is not really "used in relation to" a patient's treatment.

In March 2026 a patient advocacy group filed a complaint about TriageAssist with the Texas Attorney General. As of mid-April 2026 the Attorney General has not sent Lone Star any notice. There is no evidence that TriageAssist was developed or deployed with an intent to discriminate against any group or to cause harm. Lone Star has no AI governance program and has not adopted the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.
