## Facts

Parkline Hotels Group operates nine hotels in Manhattan and Brooklyn and employs about 2,400 people in New York City. Parkline uses "ShiftFit," a tool licensed from Tellurian HR, to score applicants for front-desk and housekeeping positions. Applicants who score below 55 are rejected automatically.

A bias audit of ShiftFit was completed in October 2025 by Whitcombe & Lyle, an auditing firm that has no financial or employment ties to Parkline or Tellurian HR and had no role in building the tool. The audit covers sex, race/ethnicity, and intersectional categories and reports selection rates and impact ratios.

Parkline posted a summary of the audit on its careers page in November 2025. The posted summary did not state ShiftFit's distribution date and did not state how many assessed individuals fell within an unknown category. The summary could be reached only through an unlabeled link in the page footer titled "Legal." In January 2026 Parkline redesigned its careers page and removed the summary entirely. Parkline has continued to use ShiftFit every week since then.

Parkline publishes its job postings in English and in Spanish. Its notice about ShiftFit is written only in English. The notice names ShiftFit and lists the qualifications the tool assesses, and it is emailed to each candidate four business days before the candidate is screened. The notice gives no instructions for requesting an alternative selection process or accommodation. Parkline's website provides no information about the data ShiftFit collects, the source of that data, or Parkline's retention policy for it.
