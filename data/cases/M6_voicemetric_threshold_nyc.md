# M6 — VoiceMetric and the AEDT Threshold

## Metadata
- jurisdiction: NYC
- doctrinal_area: AEDT definition (substantially assist), reliance on a pooled-data bias audit
- statutes: NYC LL 144 §20-870 et seq., DCWP Final Rules
- tier: medium
- role: training
- parties: Brightwater Media, VoiceMetric, Sonara Labs, ScriptEase, Meridian Audit Group

## Facts

Brightwater Media is a publisher in Manhattan with 1,100 employees. Since June 2025 it has used two tools when hiring editorial assistants for its New York City office.

"ScriptEase" converts the audio of recorded interviews into written transcripts. It produces no score, ranking, or recommendation.

"VoiceMetric," licensed from Sonara Labs, analyzes the same recordings and gives each candidate a score from 1 to 10 for "communication" and "role alignment." Brightwater's written hiring rubric combines four criteria: the VoiceMetric score (40%), a writing test (30%), résumé review (20%), and reference checks (10%). A hiring manager makes the final decision.

Brightwater's position is that VoiceMetric is not an automated employment decision tool: "A human makes every hiring decision, and the score is only one of four factors."

Even so, Brightwater points to a bias audit as a precaution. Sonara Labs commissioned the audit from Meridian Audit Group, a firm with no financial or employment ties to Sonara or Brightwater and no role in building the tool. It was completed in August 2025 and used historical data pooled from twelve other employers that use VoiceMetric. Brightwater did not give the auditor any data from its own use of the tool, although by April 2026 it has ten months of such data.

Brightwater posted a summary of that audit and VoiceMetric's distribution date in a clearly labeled section of its careers page. Each job posting states, at least ten business days before screening, that VoiceMetric will be used and which qualifications it assesses, and explains how to request an alternative selection process.

## Question

Assess Brightwater's compliance position under applicable US AI law as of April 2026. Specifically address: (1) Whether VoiceMetric is an "automated employment decision tool," applying each part of the "substantially assist or replace discretionary decision making" test; (2) Whether ScriptEase is covered; (3) Whether Brightwater may rely on an audit built from other employers' historical data; (4) Whether the audit is current, and when a new one is needed; (5) Whether Brightwater's publication and notice practices are sufficient, and what obligations remain. Use the F-I-R-A-C-O structure.
