# E8 — AnalystRank Auditor Independence

## Facts

Castellan Capital is an asset manager with 650 employees in Midtown Manhattan. To hire junior analysts, Castellan uses "AnalystRank," a tool licensed from Orbis Talent Labs that ranks applicants by predicted job performance. Castellan's recruiters interview only applicants ranked in the top 15%; everyone else is rejected without further review.

Castellan relies on a bias audit of AnalystRank performed in December 2025 by Dr. Elena Voss of Voss Quantitative Consulting. In 2024, Orbis Talent Labs retained Dr. Voss to design the scoring features that AnalystRank still uses. Dr. Voss continues to hold stock options in Orbis Talent Labs.

The audit report contains selection rates for men and for women. It does not report results for race/ethnicity categories or for intersectional categories. It does not calculate impact ratios for any category. It does not state how many individuals were assessed in each category, or how many were left out of the calculations because they fell within an unknown category.

Castellan posted a summary of this audit, together with AnalystRank's distribution date, in a clearly labeled section of its careers page. It emails each candidate a notice at least ten business days before screening. The notice names the tool, identifies the qualifications it assesses, and explains how to request an alternative selection process.

Castellan's chief compliance officer considers the audit valid because "Dr. Voss is the person who understands the model best."
