## Applications (15)

### A1

Section 20-874 applies LL 144 based on candidate and position location, not employer/agency location. StaffBridge screens NYC-resident candidates for NYC-located positions. Its NJ headquarters is irrelevant. StaffBridge is an employment agency screening on behalf of employers, squarely within Section 20-874(2).

- **Rule ref:** R4
- **Fact refs:** F1, F2, F5, F6
- **Issue ref:** I1
- **Result:** satisfied

### A2

MatchPoint is derived from machine learning, issues simplified output (0-100 score and ranking), and is used to screen candidates for employment. Under DCWP Rules Section 5-300(i), StaffBridge relies solely on the simplified output since only top-10 scored candidates advance and all others are never contacted. Every element of the AEDT definition is met.

- **Rule ref:** R1
- **Fact refs:** F3, F4
- **Issue ref:** I2
- **Result:** satisfied

### A3

MatchPoint output is the sole determinant of which candidates advance. Only top-10 scored applicants are forwarded. This satisfies DCWP Rules Section 5-300(i) - relying solely on simplified output with no other factors considered.

- **Rule ref:** R2
- **Fact refs:** F4
- **Issue ref:** I2
- **Result:** satisfied

### A4

No bias audit has ever been conducted. Corvane internal bias-testing does not qualify as a bias audit by an independent auditor. The obligation under Section 20-871(a) falls on the employer or employment agency using the AEDT, not on the vendor. StaffBridge has used MatchPoint for 19 months without any compliant bias audit.

- **Rule ref:** R5
- **Fact refs:** F7, F9
- **Issue ref:** I3
- **Result:** violated

### A5

Even if Corvane had conducted a formal audit, Corvane as the developer/vendor cannot serve as its own independent auditor under Section 20-870. The independent auditor definition excludes persons involved in developing the AEDT or having a financial interest in the vendor. The vendors job argument fails both legally and factually.

- **Rule ref:** R13
- **Fact refs:** F7, F9
- **Issue ref:** I6
- **Result:** violated

### A6

No bias audit summary, distribution date, or any audit-related information appears on StaffBridge website. Section 20-871(a)(2) and Section 20-873 require public availability of audit results prior to AEDT use. Complete non-compliance.

- **Rule ref:** R6
- **Fact refs:** F8
- **Issue ref:** I4
- **Result:** violated

### A7

DCWP Rules Section 5-303 requires published results to include audit date, data source explanation, unknown category counts, applicant numbers, selection/scoring rates, and impact ratios. None of these elements are present on StaffBridge website.

- **Rule ref:** R7
- **Fact refs:** F8
- **Issue ref:** I4
- **Result:** violated

### A8

Section 20-872 requires notice to NYC-resident candidates at least 10 business days before AEDT use. Notice must inform candidates of AEDT use, identify qualifications assessed, and inform of right to request alternative process. StaffBridge provides no notice whatsoever. All three notice elements are violated.

- **Rule ref:** R8
- **Fact refs:** F10
- **Issue ref:** I5
- **Result:** violated

### A9

DCWP Rules Section 5-304(a) requires notice to include instructions for requesting alternative selection process or reasonable accommodation. No such instructions are provided to any applicant.

- **Rule ref:** R9
- **Fact refs:** F10
- **Issue ref:** I5
- **Result:** violated

### A10

DCWP Rules Section 5-304(d) requires website disclosure of AEDT data retention policy, data types collected, and data sources. StaffBridge website contains no such information.

- **Rule ref:** R10
- **Fact refs:** F11
- **Issue ref:** I5
- **Result:** violated

### A11

TRAIGA defines consumer to exclude individuals in employment context. StaffBridge screens job applicants who are in an employment context. No Texas nexus exists - StaffBridge is in NJ, positions are in NYC. TRAIGA consumer-facing requirements do not apply. Even if Texas nexus existed, TRAIGA imposes no audit, notice, or assessment requirements on private employers.

- **Rule ref:** R14
- **Fact refs:** F1, F2, F5
- **Issue ref:** I7
- **Result:** satisfied

### A12

Colorado AIA is not effective until June 30, 2026. Analysis date is April 16, 2026. Additionally, no Colorado nexus exists - StaffBridge is in NJ, positions are in NYC, no Colorado candidates or operations identified. The law is inapplicable both temporally and jurisdictionally.

- **Rule ref:** R16
- **Fact refs:** F1, F13
- **Issue ref:** I8
- **Result:** satisfied

### A13

Each use of non-compliant AEDT to screen a single candidate may be a separate violation. In February 2026 alone, 1,340 NYC candidates were screened. Multiple violation categories per candidate (no audit, no disclosure, no notice) compound exposure. At $500-$1500 per violation, monthly exposure for February alone could range from $2M to $6M.

- **Rule ref:** R11
- **Fact refs:** F6, F7, F8, F10
- **Issue ref:** I9
- **Result:** violated

### A14

DCWP Rules Section 5-301(a) prohibits use of AEDT if more than one year has passed since most recent bias audit. No bias audit has ever been conducted, so this prohibition has been violated continuously since StaffBridge began using MatchPoint for NYC candidates.

- **Rule ref:** R17
- **Fact refs:** F7, F12
- **Issue ref:** I3
- **Result:** violated

### A15

When StaffBridge does conduct a bias audit, it must use historical data per DCWP Rules Section 5-302(a). With 19 months of use, StaffBridge has statistically significant historical data and may not rely on test data alone. StaffBridge must provide its historical data to the independent auditor.

- **Rule ref:** R12
- **Fact refs:** F12
- **Issue ref:** I3
- **Result:** violated
