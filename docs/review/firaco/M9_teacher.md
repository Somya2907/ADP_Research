## Applications (14)

### A1

CareerGauge produces a score (simplified output) and only the top-scoring third advances, meaning the score is relied on solely for selection. This meets DCWP Section 5-300(i) definition of AEDT. The tool is used for employment decisions (screening and promotion) in NYC.

- **Rule ref:** R14
- **Fact refs:** F2, F3
- **Issue ref:** I1
- **Result:** satisfied

### A2

Section 20-871(c) expressly permits a bias audit to be conducted for a group of positions. The Bank grouped analyst hiring and branch manager promotion in one audit. This is permissible under the statute.

- **Rule ref:** R2
- **Fact refs:** F4, F2
- **Issue ref:** I1
- **Result:** satisfied

### A3

Section 20-871(d)(2) requires a new bias audit after any substantial modification including retraining on new or updated data. Trellis retrained CareerGauge in July 2025 on updated data and added new scoring features. This is explicitly a substantial modification. No new audit was performed. The May 2025 audit was invalidated.

- **Rule ref:** R3
- **Fact refs:** F7, F5
- **Issue ref:** I2
- **Result:** violated

### A4

Although the May 2025 audit was less than one year old in March 2026 (satisfying the calendar test), the July 2025 retraining triggered a mandatory new audit under Section 20-871(d)(2). Without a post-retraining audit, the Bank used CareerGauge in March 2026 without a valid bias audit.

- **Rule ref:** R1
- **Fact refs:** F5, F7, F8
- **Issue ref:** I3
- **Result:** violated

### A5

For the June 2026 promotion cycle, the May 2025 audit will also exceed the one-year recency limit (more than 12 months). Combined with the retraining invalidation, the audit fails on both grounds. The Bank cannot use CareerGauge for June 2026 promotions without a new audit.

- **Rule ref:** R4
- **Fact refs:** F5, F7, F9
- **Issue ref:** I3
- **Result:** violated

### A6

Notice emailed Monday March 2; screening began Thursday March 12. Counting business days: March 3 (day 1), March 4 (day 2), March 5 (day 3), March 6 (day 4), March 9 (day 5), March 10 (day 6), March 11 (day 7). Only 7 business days elapsed before use on March 12. This falls short of the required 10 business days under DCWP Section 5-304(b).

- **Rule ref:** R6
- **Fact refs:** F11
- **Issue ref:** I4
- **Result:** violated

### A7

The March 2026 candidate notice names the tool, lists the qualifications it assesses, and explains how to request an alternative process. This satisfies Section 20-872(a)(1)(i)-(ii) and DCWP Section 5-304(a) content requirements.

- **Rule ref:** R7
- **Fact refs:** F12
- **Issue ref:** I5
- **Result:** satisfied

### A8

Section 20-872(a)(1)(ii) requires the notice to identify the job qualifications and characteristics the AEDT will assess. The promotion policy does not identify these qualifications. This is a substantive content deficiency that renders the promotion notice non-compliant regardless of timing.

- **Rule ref:** R7
- **Fact refs:** F13
- **Issue ref:** I6
- **Result:** violated

### A9

The written policy was distributed in January 2026, well more than 10 business days before the June 2026 promotion cycle. Timing is satisfied under DCWP Section 5-304(c)(1). However, the content deficiency identified in A8 independently renders the notice non-compliant.

- **Rule ref:** R8
- **Fact refs:** F13, F9
- **Issue ref:** I6
- **Result:** partial

### A10

The posted summary reflects the May 2025 audit of the pre-retraining model. After the July 2025 retraining, this summary no longer reflects the most recent required bias audit. Section 5-303(a) requires posting the results of the most recent bias audit before use. The published results are stale.

- **Rule ref:** R10
- **Fact refs:** F10, F7
- **Issue ref:** I3
- **Result:** violated

### A11

Under Section 20-875, each use of a non-compliant AEDT to screen a single candidate is a separate violation at $500-$1,500. The March 2026 hiring cycle involved screening candidates without a valid bias audit (A4) and with insufficient notice timing (A6). Each candidate screened constitutes at least two separate violations. Exposure scales with candidate volume.

- **Rule ref:** R9
- **Fact refs:** F8, F11
- **Issue ref:** I7
- **Result:** violated

### A12

Colorado AIA does not take effect until June 30, 2026. No current legal obligation exists. Whether future obligations apply depends on whether the Bank does business in Colorado and deploys high-risk AI systems affecting Colorado consumers in consequential decisions. Facts are insufficient to determine Colorado nexus.

- **Rule ref:** R12
- **Fact refs:** F15
- **Issue ref:** I8
- **Result:** requires-fact

### A13

No facts indicate the Bank operates in Texas or screens Texas residents. Even if it did, TRAIGA imposes no audit, notice, or impact assessment requirements on private employers. Only intentional discrimination is prohibited. No TRAIGA violation is indicated.

- **Rule ref:** R13
- **Fact refs:** F16
- **Issue ref:** I8
- **Result:** satisfied

### A14

CareerGauge scores candidates, so DCWP Section 5-301(c) applies. The audit reports selection rates and impact ratios but the facts describe selection rates rather than scoring rates. Section 5-301(c) requires calculating median score, scoring rate, and impact ratio. The audit may not have used the correct methodology for a scoring AEDT. This is a potential additional deficiency.

- **Rule ref:** R5
- **Fact refs:** F3, F6
- **Issue ref:** I1
- **Result:** partial
