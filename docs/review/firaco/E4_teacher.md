## Applications (9)

### A1

LL 144 Section 20-874 applies based on candidate location, not employer location. 212 NYC-resident applicants were screened for remote positions. Northlake's lack of NYC office is irrelevant. LL 144 applies to Northlake's screening of these candidates.

- **Rule ref:** R1
- **Fact refs:** F1, F2, F5
- **Issue ref:** I1
- **Result:** satisfied

### A2

FitScreen produces a numerical score (simplified output) from video interviews using AI/ML. Under DCWP Rule 5-300(i), relying solely on a simplified output with no other factors considered constitutes substantially assisting or replacing discretionary decision making. Applicants below 60 are auto-rejected with no human review. FitScreen is an AEDT.

- **Rule ref:** R2
- **Fact refs:** F3, F4
- **Issue ref:** I2
- **Result:** satisfied

### A3

The September 2025 audit is substantively adequate and within one year for post-September 2025 use. However, FitScreen was used for NYC applicants starting June 2025, potentially three months before any audit existed. Pre-audit use may constitute separate violations.

- **Rule ref:** R3
- **Fact refs:** F6, F7
- **Issue ref:** I3
- **Result:** partial

### A4

Ardent Assurance LLP has no relationship with Northlake or Pallas Analytics and played no part in building FitScreen. This satisfies the independent auditor requirement of Section 20-870.

- **Rule ref:** R4
- **Fact refs:** F6
- **Issue ref:** I3
- **Result:** satisfied

### A5

Section 20-871(a)(2) requires public posting of bias audit summary and distribution date on employer website before AEDT use. Northlake kept the audit entirely internal. No summary, no results data, and no distribution date appear on the website. This is a clear violation making every use of FitScreen for NYC candidates unlawful.

- **Rule ref:** R5
- **Fact refs:** F8
- **Issue ref:** I4
- **Result:** violated

### A6

Section 20-872 requires notice at least 10 business days before AEDT use identifying: (i) AEDT use, (ii) characteristics assessed, (iii) alternative process instructions. The generic statement about 'modern assessment technology' fails all three elements. No 10-business-day advance notice was provided. Complete violation on all notice requirements.

- **Rule ref:** R6
- **Fact refs:** F9, F10
- **Issue ref:** I5
- **Result:** violated

### A7

Colorado AIA does not take effect until June 30, 2026. As of April 16, 2026, no obligations are enforceable. Additionally, no facts establish Northlake does business in Colorado. Not applicable on current facts.

- **Rule ref:** R8
- **Fact refs:** F13, F14
- **Issue ref:** I6
- **Result:** requires-fact

### A8

TRAIGA prohibits only intentional discrimination. No facts indicate Northlake deployed FitScreen with discriminatory intent. TRAIGA's consumer provisions exclude employment contexts per Section 551.001(2). No TRAIGA violation identified.

- **Rule ref:** R9
- **Fact refs:** F3, F4, F14
- **Issue ref:** I7
- **Result:** satisfied

### A9

Each screening of a single NYC-resident candidate with a non-compliant AEDT may be a separate violation. With 212 candidates and at least two independent violation categories (disclosure and notice), potential exposure is 424+ violations at $500-$1,500 each, totaling $212,000-$636,000.

- **Rule ref:** R7
- **Fact refs:** F5
- **Issue ref:** I8
- **Result:** violated
