## Applications (13)

### A1

- **Result:** satisfied

### A2

CareQueue's recommendations determine whether patients receive physical therapy and how many sessions are authorized. This has a material effect on the provision or denial of health-care services under 6-1-1701(3)(e), making these consequential decisions.

- **Rule ref:** R1
- **Fact refs:** F4
- **Issue ref:** I1
- **Result:** satisfied

### A3

Staff follow CareQueue 90% of the time and never reconsider declined referrals. Under 6-1-1701(9), human review does not negate substantial factor status when based primarily on AI output. The 90% follow rate demonstrates staff rely primarily on CareQueue's output. CareQueue is a substantial factor.

- **Rule ref:** R3
- **Fact refs:** F5
- **Issue ref:** I2
- **Result:** satisfied

### A4

CareQueue is an AI system (A1) that is a substantial factor (A3) in making consequential decisions about health-care services (A2). No exceptions in 6-1-1701(7)(a)-(d) apply: CareQueue does not perform a narrow procedural task, does not improve prior human activity, does not merely detect patterns, and is not preparatory. CareQueue is a high-risk AI system.

- **Rule ref:** R2
- **Fact refs:** F3, F4, F5
- **Issue ref:** I1
- **Result:** satisfied
