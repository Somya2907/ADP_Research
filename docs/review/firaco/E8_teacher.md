## Applications (11)

### A1

AnalystRank produces a ranking (simplified output) from a computational process. Castellan relies solely on this ranking to screen candidates — only top 15% advance, all others rejected without further review. This meets DCWP Rules Section 5-300(i): relying solely on simplified output with no other factors considered. The tool screens candidates for employment in NYC. AnalystRank is an AEDT.

- **Rule ref:** R1
- **Fact refs:** F2, F3, F12
- **Issue ref:** I1
- **Result:** satisfied

### A2

Dr. Voss fails independence on two grounds. First, she was involved in developing AnalystRank — she designed its scoring features in 2024 for Orbis Talent Labs, triggering DCWP Rules Section 5-300 disqualification criterion (i). Second, she holds stock options in Orbis Talent Labs (the vendor), constituting a direct financial interest triggering criterion (iii). The CCO rationale that she understands the model best is legally irrelevant to independence requirements.

- **Rule ref:** R2
- **Fact refs:** F5, F6, F15
- **Issue ref:** I2
- **Result:** violated

### A3

The audit fails five mandatory requirements: (1) no race/ethnicity category analysis per DCWP Rules Section 5-301(b)(3)(ii); (2) no intersectional category analysis per Section 5-301(b)(3)(iii); (3) no impact ratios calculated for any category per Section 5-301(b)(2); (4) no applicant counts stated per Section 5-301(b)(1) and Section 5-303(a)(1); (5) no unknown-category count per Section 5-301(b)(5). The audit is substantively deficient on every metric beyond basic sex-category selection rates.

- **Rule ref:** R3
- **Fact refs:** F7, F8, F9
- **Issue ref:** I3
- **Result:** violated

### A4

Castellan posted a summary and distribution date on its careers page, satisfying the procedural posting requirement of DCWP Rules Section 5-303(a). However, the posted summary necessarily omits required content (race/ethnicity data, intersectional data, impact ratios, applicant counts, unknown-category counts) because the underlying audit omits them. The disclosure is procedurally compliant but substantively deficient under Section 5-303(a)(1).

- **Rule ref:** R4
- **Fact refs:** F10, F7, F8, F9
- **Issue ref:** I4
- **Result:** partial

### A5

Castellan emails candidates at least 10 business days before screening, names the tool, identifies qualifications assessed, and explains how to request an alternative selection process. This substantially satisfies DCWP Rules Section 5-304(a)-(b). Minor gap: Section 20-872(a)(2) requires stating employer will consider other information beyond AEDT output, which may be difficult given the sole-reliance screening process.

- **Rule ref:** R5
- **Fact refs:** F11
- **Issue ref:** I4
- **Result:** satisfied

### A6

Because the bias audit is invalid (non-independent auditor and substantively deficient), Castellan is using an AEDT without a valid bias audit. Each candidate screened may constitute a separate violation under Section 20-875, with penalties of $500-$1,500 per violation.

- **Rule ref:** R6
- **Fact refs:** F2, F3
- **Issue ref:** I3
- **Result:** violated

### A7

No facts suggest Castellan deploys AnalystRank with intent to unlawfully discriminate. TRAIGA Section 551.051 requires intentional discrimination. Absent evidence of discriminatory intent, no violation exists.

- **Rule ref:** R7
- **Fact refs:** F13
- **Issue ref:** I5
- **Result:** satisfied

### A8

TRAIGA Section 551.001(2) excludes employment contexts from the consumer definition. TRAIGA imposes no bias audit, notice, impact assessment, or risk management obligations on private employers for hiring tools. Castellan has no affirmative TRAIGA obligations regarding AnalystRank.

- **Rule ref:** R8
- **Fact refs:** F13
- **Issue ref:** I5
- **Result:** satisfied

### A9

TRAIGA Section 551.201 preempts only local Texas ordinances. It has no effect on NYC LL 144. Castellan remains fully subject to NYC law for its NYC operations.

- **Rule ref:** R9
- **Fact refs:** F12
- **Issue ref:** I5
- **Result:** satisfied

### A10

Colorado AIA is not yet in force (effective June 30, 2026). If Castellan screens candidates for Colorado positions, AnalystRank would be a high-risk AI system under Section 6-1-1701(7) because it makes consequential employment decisions. Castellan would need to implement NIST AI RMF-aligned risk management, complete impact assessments, provide consumer notices, and offer data correction and appeal rights. Whether Castellan has a Colorado nexus is a factual question not resolved in the case.

- **Rule ref:** R10
- **Fact refs:** F2, F3, F14
- **Issue ref:** I6
- **Result:** requires-fact
