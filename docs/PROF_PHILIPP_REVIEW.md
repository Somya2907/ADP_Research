## What to check

1. Are the cited authorities and effective dates correct for the date stated in the case?
2. Are the rules applied correctly, with the relevant exceptions and uncertainties?
3. Do the conclusions and obligations follow from the facts and law? Please flag missing duties or unsupported assumptions.
